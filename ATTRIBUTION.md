# Media Sources & Attribution

All visual and audio assets in this project are authentic, documented, and legally licensed for educational and web usage. In accordance with strict devotional and authenticity guidelines, no AI-generated images of sacred deities or historical religious figures are used.

---

### Project-Wide Image Rules

1. **Udupi Sri Krishna (Presiding Deity of Udupi Matha):**
   - **Requirement**: ALWAYS use a real, authenticated web-sourced photograph of the sacred idol at Sri Krishna Matha in Udupi, Karnataka.
   - **Prohibitions**: Never AI-generate Udupi Sri Krishna; never substitute with idols from other temples; never use regional folk art styles (no Tanjore, Kalinga, Pichwai, Nathdwara, miniature styles).
   - **UI Cleanliness**: The frontend webpage remains clean with no visible attribution badges, text overlays, or watermarks over the sacred image. Full licensing metadata is maintained in this documentation.

2. **Generic Krishna Artwork (Throughout the site):**
   - **Aesthetic**: Serene, aesthetically pleasing, Pinterest-style devotional artwork and illustrations (e.g., Krishna playing the flute, Krishna with Radha, cows, peacocks, Vrindavan, Bhagavad Gita chariot dialogue).
   - **AI Generation**: High-quality AI-generated artwork is permitted for generic devotional illustrations unless a web-sourced image is explicitly requested.
   - **Prohibitions**: No regional folk/cultural art styles (no Tanjore, Kalinga, Pichwai, miniature) unless explicitly requested.

3. **Specific Sacred & Historical Subjects:**
   - Whenever a specific real-world deity, temple, historical philosopher (e.g. Sri Madhvacharya), or sacred landmark is requested, it must be sourced from authentic, verified real-world sources with clear licensing.
