अध्याय १ • अर्जुनविषादयोग

Arjuna Viṣāda Yoga — The Yoga of Arjuna's Dejection

As the armies assemble on Kurukshetra, Arjuna is overwhelmed by grief upon seeing his revered teachers, kinsmen, and friends. Casting down his bow Gandiva, he collapses in despondency, preparing the stage for Sri Krishna's divine discourse.

Verse:
BG 1.1 श्लोक १ • Verse 1 of 47
॥ श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता ॥

धृतराष्ट्र उवाच |
धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः |
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय || १ ||

IAST Transliteration

dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca |
dharmakṣetre kurukṣetre samavetā yuyutsavaḥ |
māmakāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva kimakurvata sañjaya || 1 ||

English Translation
"Dhritarashtra said: O Sanjaya, assembled on the sacred field of Kurukshetra, eager for battle, what did my sons and the sons of Pandu do?"
